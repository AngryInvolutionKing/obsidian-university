## 课堂示例：AI 味学术写作（论文结构段）

幻灯片原文（AI 生成的反面教材，[ ] 处被遮挡，按上下文补齐）：
> The *manuscript* **unfurls** in the following manner: the **succeeding** section **delves** into the **theoretical scaffolding** employed for the analysis, pinpointing the context of the XYZ as significant for evaluating alignment and misalignment **among** A and B. The third **segment delineates** the **espoused** methodology, elucidating both the mechanisms of data gathering and processing. The **penultimate part** reveals the discoveries and **postulates** the theoretical and managerial **connotations** of our results. The concluding [remarks] **encapsulate** the primary revelations [of the study], sketching its constraints and identifying [opportunit]y for continued exploration.

中文翻译：
> 本文稿按以下方式展开：下一节深入探讨分析所采用的理论框架，指出 XYZ 的情境对于评估 A 与 B 之间的一致与不一致具有重要意义。第三部分阐述所采用的研究方法，说明数据收集与处理的具体机制。倒数第二部分呈现研究发现，并提出结果在理论与管理层面的启示。最后部分概括研究的主要发现，勾勒研究的局限，并指出未来继续探索的方向。

花哨替换词 → 平实地道说法（老师标色词）：

| 幻灯片用词 | 平实说法 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| unfurls | presents / proceeds | 手稿不会"展开"，拟人化滥用 |
| succeeding section | the next / following section | |
| delves into | examines / covers | delve 是 ChatGPT 最著名的口头禅 |
| theoretical scaffolding | theoretical framework | 比喻滥用 |
| among A and B | **between** A and B | 语法错误：两者只能用 between |
| segment | section | |
| delineates | describes / outlines | |
| espoused methodology | the adopted methodology | espouse 指"信奉理念"，不用于方法 |
| elucidating | explaining | |
| discoveries | findings | |
| postulates the …connotations | discusses the …implications | postulate 是"假定"，不能支配 implications |
| connotations | implications | 论文里"启示"用 implications |
| encapsulate | summarize | |
| revelations | findings | revelation 太夸张 |
| sketching its constraints | outlining its limitations | |

平实版改写（论文里真正该写的样子）：
> The manuscript proceeds as follows: the next section examines the theoretical framework used for the analysis, explaining why the context of XYZ is significant for evaluating alignment and misalignment **between** A and B. The third section describes the adopted methodology, covering both data collection and processing. The penultimate section presents the findings and discusses their theoretical and managerial implications. The final section concludes by summarizing the main findings, outlining the study's limitations, and suggesting directions for future research.

要点：AI 生成文本有可识别的指纹——**同义词华丽化（thesaurus abuse）**，集中在 delve、unfurl、elucidate、encapsulate、delineate 等词。学术写作第一美德是 clarity；认出这些词既是识别 AI 痕迹的方式，也是给自己文章"去 AI 味"的修改清单。
