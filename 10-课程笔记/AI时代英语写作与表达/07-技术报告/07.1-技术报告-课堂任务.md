## 技术报告样本（课件原文）

> **Title: Design and Testing of a Prosthetic Hand with Tactile Feedback**
>
> **Abstract**
>
> This report presents the design, implementation, and testing results of a prototype prosthetic hand with tactile feedback capability. The device integrates five force sensors and a vibration motor array to provide real-time haptic feedback to the user. Testing was conducted with six participants over a four-week period. Results indicate a 32% improvement in object recognition accuracy compared to non-feedback conditions.
>
> **1. Introduction**
>
> Prosthetic hands currently on the market lack effective tactile feedback, limiting users' ability to perform fine motor tasks. This project aims to address this limitation by developing a low-cost, sensor-integrated prosthetic hand that provides real-time haptic feedback to the user.
>
> **2. Methodology**
>
> The prosthetic hand was designed using 3D-printed components and five force sensors embedded in the fingertips. A vibration motor array was integrated into the wrist section to deliver haptic feedback corresponding to grip force. Testing was conducted with six participants, who performed object recognition tasks under two conditions: with and without haptic feedback.
>
> **3. Results**
>
> Participants demonstrated a 32% improvement in object recognition accuracy when haptic feedback was enabled. Reaction time decreased by an average of 0.4 seconds.
>
> **4. Discussion and Conclusion**
>
> The results suggest that haptic feedback significantly enhances prosthetic hand functionality. Future work will focus on miniaturizing the vibration motor array and improving battery life.

## 四项分析（英文作答与中文整理）

### 1. Intended audience（目标读者）

The intended audience is likely to include biomedical engineers, prosthetic device designers, and researchers interested in rehabilitation technology.

**Evidence:** The report uses technical terms such as “force sensors,” “vibration motor array,” and “haptic feedback” without detailed explanations. This suggests that readers are expected to have some relevant technical knowledge.

**中文整理：**目标读者主要是生物医学工程师、假肢设计人员及康复技术研究者。这是根据术语使用作出的推断，原文没有直接说明读者身份。

### 2. Primary purpose（主要目的）

The primary purpose is to describe the design and testing of a prototype prosthetic hand and report how tactile feedback affects its performance. The report also identifies areas for future improvement.

**Evidence:** The abstract states, “This report presents the design, implementation, and testing results of a prototype prosthetic hand with tactile feedback capability.” The results report a “32% improvement in object recognition accuracy.”

**中文整理：**主要目的是介绍这种假手的设计与测试，并用实验结果说明触觉反馈的作用。

### 3. Structure and organization（结构与各部分功能）

The report follows a clear research-based structure:

| Section | Function | Specific evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Identifies the device and the focus of the report. | “Design and Testing of a Prosthetic Hand with Tactile Feedback” |
| Abstract | Summarizes the project, device, testing, and main finding. | Mentions five force sensors, six participants, a four-week testing period, and a 32% improvement. |
| Introduction | Explains the existing problem and states the project's aim. | Existing prosthetic hands “lack effective tactile feedback”; the project aims to develop a low-cost alternative with feedback. |
| Methodology | Describes the device's construction and the testing procedure. | Describes 3D-printed components, fingertip sensors, wrist-mounted vibration motors, and testing with and without feedback. |
| Results | Presents the main experimental findings. | Recognition accuracy improved by 32%, and reaction time decreased by an average of 0.4 seconds. |
| Discussion and Conclusion | Interprets the findings and proposes future development. | Suggests improved functionality and identifies motor miniaturization and battery life as future priorities. |

The organization moves logically from the problem and aim to the methods, findings, and implications.

**中文整理：**报告依次交代问题与目标、方法、结果及其意义；摘要概括主要信息，讨论与结论解释结果并提出后续改进方向。

### 4. Key language features（主要语言特点）

#### ① Formal and objective tone（正式、客观）

The report focuses on the device, procedures, and measurable outcomes rather than personal feelings. It avoids first-person expressions such as “I think.”

**Evidence:** “Reaction time decreased by an average of 0.4 seconds.”

#### ② Technical vocabulary（专业词汇）

Specialized vocabulary communicates the engineering design precisely.

**Evidence:** “prosthetic hand,” “force sensors,” “haptic feedback,” and “vibration motor array.”

#### ③ Passive voice（被动语态）

Passive constructions emphasize what was done rather than who performed the action.

**Evidence:** “The prosthetic hand was designed using 3D-printed components” and “Testing was conducted with six participants.”

#### ④ Different tenses for different functions（根据功能使用不同时态）

The report uses the present tense for the current problem, the project purpose, and some summary statements, the past tense for completed procedures and observed results, and the future tense for planned improvements.

**Evidence:**

- **Present:** “Prosthetic hands currently on the market lack effective tactile feedback.”
- **Past:** “A vibration motor array was integrated into the wrist section.”
- **Future:** “Future work will focus on miniaturizing the vibration motor array and improving battery life.”

#### ⑤ Quantitative and cautious language（量化表达与审慎措辞）

Numbers make the findings specific, while words such as “suggest” show caution when interpreting them.

**Evidence:** “six participants,” “32% improvement,” “0.4 seconds,” and “The results suggest…”
